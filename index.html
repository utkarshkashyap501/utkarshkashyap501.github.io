How I use AI

I use AI every single day, at work and on my own projects. The way I look at it, AI is only worth it if it saves real time or people actually use it. So here's what that looks like for me in practice.

At work

  • Built AI agents and RAG workflows for real business use cases.
  • Rolled out Copilot and Glean company-wide, and ran the training so people actually used them.
  • Kept our prompt libraries, agent catalogs and model registries in order, and tracked latency and cost.
  • Automated our team's recurring tasks with AI, which got us a 30% efficiency gain.

On my own builds

  • Built ishqiyaa.com with Lovable, and wired in Razorpay payments myself.
  • Built a job application co-pilot in n8n. It runs on my Mac with a free Groq model and checks in with me on Telegram.
  • I use Claude Code and Replit when I want to build a small tool quickly.

Every day

  • Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini for research, first drafts and pressure-testing my thinking.
  • I got into AI at Hive.ai, checking model output for a living: spotting hallucinations, running LLM-as-a-judge checks and calibrating with human graders.
  • So I never take a first answer at face value. I check it.

One rule I stick to: a human signs off. My own co-pilot doesn't send anything until I say yes.
