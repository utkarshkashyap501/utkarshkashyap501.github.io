How I work

The same loop I've run with enterprise clients and on my own builds: understand the problem, write it down clearly, ship it, then make sure people use it.

1

Discover

Client discovery workshops, customer journey mapping and market research to find the real pain point before anyone writes code.

2

Define

PRDs, BRDs and user stories with scenarios, edge cases and acceptance criteria. Wireframes and AI prototypes when words aren't enough.

3

Deliver

Backlog ownership and sprint planning in JIRA, UAT, regression checks and root cause analysis until it's ready to go live.

4

Drive adoption

Rollout, training and change management, then NPS, CSAT and usage data to see what's working and what to fix next.
